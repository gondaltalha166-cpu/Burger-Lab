Why Karachi Loves Burger Lab

Crafted with passion, served piping hot every single time

At Burger Lab, we believe that a great burger starts long before it hits the grill. Every morning, our kitchen team prepares fresh, never-frozen 100% halal prime beef cuts, grinding them to the ideal fat-to-lean ratio so every smash patty develops a crispy, caramelized crust while staying juicy on the inside.

Our signature potato brioche buns are baked fresh daily to hold up against double layers of melted aged cheddar, smoky beef bacon, and our secret house-made Burger Lab sauce. Whether you crave a classic double smash burger or a fiery Nashville hot crispy zinger, every order is cooked fresh on demand.

Looking for a late-night feast or a quick lunch combo in Clifton and across Karachi? Our fast delivery service ensures your burgers, animal loaded fries, and hand-spun Belgian chocolate shakes arrive hot and fresh at your doorstep within minutes.
